Project Name: FitTrack - Simple Exercise Guide

Description:
A simple fitness website that shows different exercises with instructions.
Users can browse exercises by category and see how to do each exercise.

Data I will store:
- Exercises: name, category, difficulty, description, image, duration
- Categories: strength, cardio, flexibility

Main Features:
- View all exercises
- Filter exercises by category
- Simple and clean design
- Easy to follow instructions
